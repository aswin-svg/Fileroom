Image Compressor

Compress JPG, PNG and WebP images to fit a size limit such as 50 KB or 100 KB for online forms. Live preview, batch ZIP download, no upload.

How It Works & FAQ

How to compress an image to a target size

  1. Add one or many JPG, PNG or WebP images.
  2. Move the quality slider, or type a target size such as 100 KB.
  3. Check the preview and estimated size, then download (a ZIP for several images).

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress an image to 100 KB?

Type 100 in the target size box. Fileroom lowers the quality, and shrinks the image if needed, until it fits.

How can I reduce a photo to 50 KB for an online form?

Enter 50 as the target size and choose JPG or WebP. Check the preview, then download.

Can I compress many images at once?

Yes. Add several images and download them together as one ZIP.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser, so your photos stay on your device.

Runtime downloads and privacy

OCR and background removal load their public runtime files from this site. Your files stay on this device.

File limits and formats

PDFs can be up to 50 MB and images up to 25 MB per file. Large pages and batches can still hit browser memory limits. Remove passwords from PDFs before processing. Low PDF compression preserves selectable text and may save little space. Medium and High can shrink scanned PDFs by converting pages to JPEGs, which removes selectable text and editable form fields.

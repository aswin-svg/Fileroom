Split PDF

Extract specific pages or split every page of a PDF into separate files. Free, private and processed in your browser.

How It Works & FAQ

How to split a PDF or extract pages

  1. Add a PDF and check its page count.
  2. Type pages such as 1-3, 5, or choose to split every page.
  3. Download the new PDF, or a ZIP with one file per page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract pages from a PDF?

Enter a range such as 1-3, 5 and press Split file. Those pages are saved into one new PDF.

Can I split every page into its own file?

Yes. Choose the split every page option and you get a ZIP with one PDF per page.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The file is split on your device and never sent to a server.

Runtime downloads and privacy

OCR and background removal load their public runtime files from this site. Your files stay on this device.

File limits and formats

PDFs can be up to 50 MB and images up to 25 MB per file. Large pages and batches can still hit browser memory limits. Remove passwords from PDFs before processing. Low PDF compression preserves selectable text and may save little space. Medium and High can shrink scanned PDFs by converting pages to JPEGs, which removes selectable text and editable form fields.

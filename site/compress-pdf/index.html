PDF Compressor

Try structural PDF optimization to keep selectable text, or convert pages to JPEG for image-based compression. Results vary by file.

How It Works & FAQ

How to reduce the size of a PDF

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Choose Low (keeps text), Medium or High (pages become images).
  3. Compare the before and after size, then download.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce the size of a scanned PDF?

Choose Medium or High. They turn each page into a compressed image, which can cut the file size a lot.

What does Low compression do?

It keeps text selectable and only cleans up the file structure, so the savings are small.

Will text stay selectable after compressing?

Only on Low. Medium and High convert pages to images, so text can no longer be selected.

Is it private?

Yes. Compression runs in your browser and your PDF is never uploaded.

Runtime downloads and privacy

OCR and background removal load their public runtime files from this site. Your files stay on this device.

File limits and formats

PDFs can be up to 50 MB and images up to 25 MB per file. Large pages and batches can still hit browser memory limits. Remove passwords from PDFs before processing. Low PDF compression preserves selectable text and may save little space. Medium and High can shrink scanned PDFs by converting pages to JPEGs, which removes selectable text and editable form fields.

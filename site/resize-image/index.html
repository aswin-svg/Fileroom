Image Resizer

Resize JPG, PNG and WebP images by pixels or percentage. Lock aspect ratio, use presets like 1080p or Instagram Square, all in your browser.

How It Works & FAQ

How to resize an image online

  1. Add your images and check their original size.
  2. Enter a width and height, or tap a preset such as 50% or 1080p.
  3. Download the resized image, or a ZIP for several.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image without stretching it?

Keep Lock aspect ratio on. Typing a width fills in the matching height.

What size is an Instagram square post?

1080 × 1080 pixels. The Instagram Square preset crops from the center to fill it.

Can I resize by percentage?

Yes. The 50% and 75% presets scale every image to that share of its original size.

Runtime downloads and privacy

OCR and background removal load their public runtime files from this site. Your files stay on this device.

File limits and formats

PDFs can be up to 50 MB and images up to 25 MB per file. Large pages and batches can still hit browser memory limits. Remove passwords from PDFs before processing. Low PDF compression preserves selectable text and may save little space. Medium and High can shrink scanned PDFs by converting pages to JPEGs, which removes selectable text and editable form fields.

Watermark

Add a text watermark to PDFs or images with adjustable placement, color, size and opacity, directly in your browser.

How It Works & FAQ

How to add a text watermark

  1. Add one or more PDFs or images.
  2. Enter your text and choose its position, color, size and opacity.
  3. Apply the watermark and download your files.

Frequently asked questions

Does watermarking make PDF text unselectable?

Existing PDF text remains selectable. The watermark is added as text.

Can I watermark a batch of files?

Yes. Add the files, choose your watermark settings, and download the processed files together as a ZIP.

Runtime downloads and privacy

OCR and background removal load their public runtime files from this site. Your files stay on this device.

File limits and formats

PDFs can be up to 50 MB and images up to 25 MB per file. Large pages and batches can still hit browser memory limits. Remove passwords from PDFs before processing. Low PDF compression preserves selectable text and may save little space. Medium and High can shrink scanned PDFs by converting pages to JPEGs, which removes selectable text and editable form fields.

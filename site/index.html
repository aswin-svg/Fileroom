How It Works & FAQ

How Fileroom works

  1. Pick a tool from the grid or the Tools menu.
  2. Drop in your files. They are processed on your device.
  3. Download the result, or a ZIP if you processed several images.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser, so your PDFs and images never leave your device.

Is Fileroom free to use?

Yes. There is no sign-up. Fileroom adds a watermark only when you use the Watermark tool.

Which tools are included?

PDF Combiner, Split PDF, PDF Compressor, Image Compressor, Image Resizer, Image Format Converter, PDF to JPG, JPG to PDF, Watermark, Background Remover, Organize PDF, Crop Image, OCR / Extract Text and Sign PDF.

Runtime downloads and privacy

OCR and background removal load their public runtime files from this site. Your files stay on this device.

File limits and formats

PDFs can be up to 50 MB and images up to 25 MB per file. Large pages and batches can still hit browser memory limits. Remove passwords from PDFs before processing. Low PDF compression preserves selectable text and may save little space. Medium and High can shrink scanned PDFs by converting pages to JPEGs, which removes selectable text and editable form fields.
